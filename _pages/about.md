Hi there! I’m Xuemei. 👋

I earned my PhD from Peking University in 2024, under the supervision of [Prof. Jun Wang](https://pkudh.org/member/wangjun2025.html) and  [Prof. Qi Su](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=9f4JUrUAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN), and I’m currently a postdoctoral researcher at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, working with [Prof. Chu-Ren Huang](https://churenhuang.com/) and [Dr. Jinghang Gu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=b7FMqr4AAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao).

My research lies at the intersection of Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Digital Humanities, where I explore how computational models can help us better understand language, culture, and history.

I’m currently seeking postdoctoral or assistant professor opportunities for 2026 year.
